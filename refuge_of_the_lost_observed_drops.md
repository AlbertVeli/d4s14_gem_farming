# Refuge of the Lost: Observed Drops

## Layout

The Nightmare Dungeon has two sections. The main route consideration is that a Whirlwind build can simply spin to win through the first section, while the second requires careful navigation.

1. A narrow tunnel. Slay all enemies; a Whirlwind build can spin to win through it without slowing down.
2. A tunnel in a circular formation, with zero or one possible wrong turn depending on RNG. Avoid that wrong turn, then free five bound prisoners.

Freeing the five prisoners is part of the route.

The map-pin trick helps avoid the wrong turn in the second section, which can otherwise lead to a random event instead of the boss room.

There are two bosses instead of one: Molten Conjurer and Galvanized Mauler.

## Observed Royal Gem Drops

Like Seer's Reach, Refuge of the Lost guarantees **three Royal-or-better gems** per run. Its clear time is comparable: freeing the five prisoners makes it about five seconds slower, but the slightly smaller dungeon makes up for some of that difference.
